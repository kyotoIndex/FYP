5 Future Directions
5.1 Computation and Communication Overhead

computational and storage overhead of local clients. FL faces significant communication challenges
as it requires frequent exchanges of model information (parameters or gradients) among distributed
clients and services. When it comes to using FL for LLM, the communication overhead becomes
even more significant, with gigabit-level data transmissions necessary to achieve centralized training
performance. This level of communication overhead is not acceptable for FL systems. Furthermore,
