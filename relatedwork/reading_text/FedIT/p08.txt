Simulated Local Training This core component of our Fed-PEIT framework is implemented in
the fed_util/client.py module. In real-world scenarios, all selected clients perform their local
training simultaneously, which can be computationally expensive to simulate. To make it feasible for
researchers with limited resources, our framework conducts the local training of clients sequentially,
one at a time. To implement the LoRA method, we utilize the PEFT package [53] and the Alpaca-lora
repository 9 to encapsulate the frozen, original pre-trained model with LoRA configurations, enabling
more efficient parameter-efficient fine-tuning for our Shepherd framework.
