Figure 6: Detailed visualization of the proposed AS-LoRA score computation and mode selection
procedures. (a) Component-wise scores are computed from the projected gradients of LoRA A and
B matrices. Random projection mitigates the effect of channel-wise gradient outliers before applying
the squared-gradient and curvature-aware score terms. (b) The server smooths the aggregated scores
using EMA and performs layer-wise mode selection. During warm-up, AS-LoRA alternates between
components, while after warm-up it uses a temperature-scaled score-based selection rule to produce
the final mode vector.
