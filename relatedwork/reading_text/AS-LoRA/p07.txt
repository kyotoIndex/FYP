practical, we consider two approximation strategies, Hessian-
Vector Products (HVP) [38] and Finite-Difference (FD) [ 39] estimation, which are described in

based scoring yields competitive results without explicitly forming or storing the Hessian matrix. We
adopt the FD-based criterion in all subsequent experiments, as it preserves the curvature-aware nature
of the proposed score while maintaining practical computational cost.
