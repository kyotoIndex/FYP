problem aims to maximize the total value of selected items while
ensuring that their combined weight does not exceed a predefined
capacity. In this context, value corresponds to the utility or benefit
of an item, while weight represents its associated cost or resource
consumption. The capacity defines the maximum allowable total
weight for the selected items.
