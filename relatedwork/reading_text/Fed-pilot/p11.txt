Supplement
We provide more discussions and experiments of this work in the supplement and organize it as follows:
• Supplement A: Additional details on memory consumption in LoRA-based FM fine-tuning.
– In Subsection A.1, we provide an example of how to calculate activations with only a frozen linear layer.
– In Subsection A.2, we provide an example of how to calculate activations with a frozen non-linear layer.
– In Subsection A.3, we provide details on the type of activation in the ViT model.
– In Subsection A.4, we provide a code of how to calculate memory consumption for a given LoRA allocation map with ViT-base.
• Supplement B: We provide detailed information on the experimental settings, including the models, FL settings, GPU memory capacity
configurations for each client and model level, the resources and infrastructure used for the experiments, and a detailed explanation
of the experiments shown in Figure 1.
– In Subsection B.1, we outline the FL-related settings and hyperparameters.
– In Subsection B.2, we describe the capacity chooses for each model.
– In Subsection B.3, we provide a detailed explanation of the experimental settings used for Figure 1.
• Supplement C: Details on the preprocessing of all datasets are provided.
• Supplement D: Additional results are presented in tables.
• Supplement E: A comparative study on state-of-the-art methods is provided.
• Supplement F: The limitations of our work are discussed.
• Supplement G: Directions for future work are outlined.
