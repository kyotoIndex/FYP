Overall, the relative disagreement between the two LoRA
factors depends on the realized client partition and varies
across Transformer layers and projection types. Therefore,
neither the Dirichlet concentration parameter nor an average
factor-level trend is sufficient to prescribe a fixed sharing
side across federated settings. RSS addresses this issue by
assessing, for the current client partition and target LoRA
rankr, whether a shared rank-rinput subspace is sufficient
for the local data distributions. These results support select-
ing the sharing side according to the observed client partition
rather than applying a fixed factor-sharing policy.
