6 Evaluation
6.1 Data Heterogeneity
Fig. 3 and 6 show the impact of data heterogeneity
on the performance of two models, DistilBERT
and Albert, trained on 20News and News cate-
gory datasets. The gap between the PEFT and FFT
grows in all four settings by making the data more
non-IID. This phenomenon indicates the necessity
of a new PEFT algorithm tailored for FL.
6.2 Update Size
Table 2 shows the impact of update size for the
20News group dataset on the Albert model on the
final performance of the global model. As expected,
larger update sizes can help the performance and
give a model with higher accuracy. But, the prob-
lem with this approach is that now the update size
and communication cost of training would also in-
crease, which is not desired.
