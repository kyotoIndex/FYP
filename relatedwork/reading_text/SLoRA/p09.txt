have proposed a novel approach called SLoRA that
can maintain the same performance as FFT with
minimal communication, time, and storage cost.
Limitations
In this work, we assumed all the clients have sim-
ilar resource limitations and power. However, in
the real world, some users may have more power
and can larger update sizes both for training and
inference, while others may be more constrained.
One interesting future work is extending this work
to also consider resource heterogeneity.

problems.
