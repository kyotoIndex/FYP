3 FedSAIL
In Section 2, we demonstrate that parameter similarity alone is an unreliable proxy for client agreement,
whereas the action geometry retains a robust and task-conditioned structure. Motivated by these, we propose
Federated Subspace-Guided Action-Informed Learning(FedSAIL). Instead of directly averaging adapter
weights, FedSAIL extracts and shares the dominant right subspace of the input-aware action matrix. This
allows the server to compute a basis-invariant geometric consensus to regularize local training, while keeping
both LoRA factors and client-specific residual directions strictly local.
