G Natural Language Task Performance
Figure 7 illustrates the performance of global models on natural language tasks when trained with
FlexLoRA in the FedIT setting. In line with results from the FedAvg setting, global models that
leverage heterogeneous ranks in the FedIT setting also perform better on tasks involving logical rela-
tionships between sentences, such as overlap extraction, textual entailment, cause-effect classification,
and dialogue act recognition. However, the global models underperform in word-level analysis tasks,
such as word analogy and keyword tagging. We hypothesize that this underperformance in the FedIT
setting is due to the local clients being trained with the Adam optimizer rather than the conventional
SGD optimizer. The top decoding block of the LLaMA transformer, which processes word-level
information, likely overfits specific information because of the momentum component inherent to the
Adam optimizer.
